# Goods Not Received Policy

## Purpose
This policy applies when a customer claims that a purchased item was never delivered.

## Required Evidence
**Customer must provide:**
* Order confirmation
* Proof of payment
* Communication with merchant (if available)

**Merchant may provide:**
* Delivery confirmation
* Tracking information
* Signature proof

## Investigation Process
1.  Verify transaction exists.
2.  Verify order details.
3.  Request delivery proof from merchant.
4.  Review tracking information.

## Resolution Rules
* **If merchant provides valid proof of delivery:**
    * *Recommendation:* Reject dispute.
* **If merchant cannot provide proof of delivery:**
    * *Recommendation:* Approve chargeback.

## Risk Factors
* High-value purchases
* Repeated claims from same customer
* Missing shipment records

## Analyst Notes
Analyst should verify delivery evidence before final decision.
