# Goods Not Received Policy

## Definition
A "Goods Not Received" dispute arises when a cardholder makes a legitimate payment for physical products or digital goods but never receives the item(s) they purchased. The charge is authorized and the merchant is real, but delivery never occurred. This policy covers situations where the goods were never shipped, were lost in transit, were delivered to the wrong address, or where the tracking information shows delivery but the cardholder genuinely did not receive the package.

## Qualifying Conditions
A dispute qualifies as goods not received when:
- The cardholder placed a legitimate order and was charged, but the item never arrived
- The tracking number provided by the merchant shows the package was never shipped or is stuck in transit
- The estimated delivery date has passed by more than 5 business days with no delivery
- The merchant confirms shipment but the cardholder reports non-delivery
- The carrier confirms the package was lost in transit
- The package was marked as delivered but the cardholder did not receive it
- The merchant stopped communicating after receiving payment
- A digital product (software license, e-book, streaming access) was never delivered or made accessible

## Detection Signals
The following keywords and phrases in a complaint strongly indicate goods not received:
- "I never received my order"
- "Item never arrived"
- "Package never delivered"
- "Tracking shows it was never shipped"
- "Merchant stopped responding"
- "Order was placed but nothing was sent"
- "Still waiting for my item"
- "The delivery date passed and nothing arrived"
- "Shows delivered but I never got it"
- "Seller disappeared after I paid"

## Distinction from Other Dispute Types
Goods not received is distinct from:
- **Merchant fraud:** If the seller was fake or the website was fraudulent, the dispute should be classified as Merchant Fraud
- **Service not provided:** This policy applies to physical or digital goods. Services (gym memberships, repairs, consulting) fall under Service Not Provided
- **Unauthorized transaction:** The cardholder authorized this purchase. The problem is non-delivery, not non-authorization

## Resolution Process
1. Cardholder reports the non-delivery after the expected delivery date has passed
2. Bank advises the cardholder to contact the merchant directly first (required step)
3. If the cardholder has contacted the merchant with no resolution within 5 business days, the bank opens a formal dispute
4. Bank requests proof of delivery from the merchant (signed delivery confirmation, carrier tracking showing delivery)
5. Merchant has 10 business days to provide proof of delivery
6. If proof of delivery is not provided or is insufficient, a chargeback is filed and the cardholder is credited
7. If the merchant provides valid proof of delivery, the cardholder is asked to file a claim with the carrier (for lost packages) and the bank may still pursue the chargeback on their behalf
8. Resolution is communicated to the cardholder in writing

## Investigation Timeline
- Initial review: within 2 business days
- Merchant contact period: 5–10 business days (cardholder should attempt this first)
- Bank dispute opened: after merchant fails to resolve within 5 business days
- Merchant response window for proof of delivery: 10 business days
- Credit issued (if no proof of delivery): 3–5 business days after merchant deadline
- Full resolution: 30–60 days

## Required Documentation from Customer
- Order confirmation email (showing item, quantity, and amount paid)
- Tracking number and carrier tracking history (showing non-delivery or non-shipment)
- Screenshots of any communication with the merchant (emails, chat logs, social media)
- Screenshot of the merchant's order status page (if accessible)
- Delivery address confirmation (to verify the correct address was provided)
- For digital goods: confirmation that access credentials were never received or that the download link was invalid

## Customer Rights and Obligations
- The cardholder must attempt to resolve the issue with the merchant before the bank can formally open a chargeback
- Disputes should be filed within 120 days of the expected delivery date
- If the package is shown as delivered by the carrier but the cardholder did not receive it, the cardholder must also file a claim with the carrier (USPS, FedEx, UPS, DHL)
- The cardholder must be available to respond to bank inquiries within 10 business days
- If the package is found after the dispute is resolved, the cardholder is obligated to notify the bank

## Carrier Claim Requirement
When a carrier (USPS, FedEx, UPS, DHL) shows a package as delivered but the cardholder did not receive it:
1. The cardholder must file a lost package claim with the carrier
2. The carrier claim reference number should be provided to the bank
3. The bank will pursue the chargeback from the merchant in parallel
4. The cardholder will be credited regardless of the carrier claim outcome, as the merchant bears responsibility for ensuring successful delivery

## Merchant Proof of Delivery Standards
For a merchant's proof of delivery to be considered valid, it must include:
- Carrier name and tracking number
- Delivery date and time
- Delivery address matching the cardholder's billing/shipping address
- Signature confirmation (for orders over $200)

Tracking that shows "in transit" or "out for delivery" does not constitute proof of successful delivery.

## Common Scenarios and Outcomes
- **Package never shipped (no tracking movement):** Chargeback filed immediately. Credit issued within 10 business days.
- **Package lost in transit:** Carrier claim opened. Bank files chargeback against merchant. Credit issued within 15–30 days.
- **Shows delivered, cardholder denies receipt:** Bank investigates delivery address. If address matches, carrier claim required. Bank credit issued within 30 days.
- **Merchant claims item was shipped but cannot provide tracking:** Chargeback upheld. Credit issued within 10 business days.
- **Digital goods never delivered:** Bank confirms non-delivery by requesting evidence from the cardholder. Credit issued within 5–10 business days.
