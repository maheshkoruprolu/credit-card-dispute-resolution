# Service Not Provided Policy

## Purpose
This policy applies when a cardholder claims they paid for a service (e.g., travel, memberships, professional services, event tickets) that was canceled, not performed, or fundamentally unfulfilled by the merchant.

## Required Evidence
**Customer must provide:**
* Service contract, booking confirmation, or ticket stub showing scheduled dates.
* Proof of service cancellation (e.g., flight cancellation email, gym closure notice).
* Evidence of an attempt to resolve the issue directly with the merchant.

**Merchant may provide:**
* Attendance logs, check-in records, or digital access timestamps proving the customer utilized the service.
* Signed waiver or terms of service showing the customer accepted non-refundable status or failed to show up (No-Show).
* Evidence that a comparable alternative service or voucher was issued and accepted by the customer.

## Investigation Process
1.  **Date Verification:** Verify the agreed-upon date of service provision. Ensure the dispute is filed after the service date has passed.
2.  **Terms Review:** Read the cancellation policy and merchant terms agreed to at checkout.
3.  **Merchant Contact Verification:** Assess whether the merchant was given a fair opportunity to remedy the issue or offer a refund.

## Resolution Rules
* **If the merchant proves the customer checked in, logged in, or physically attended:**
    * *Recommendation:* Reject dispute.
* **If the service was cancelled by the merchant and no refund or mutually accepted voucher was issued:**
    * *Recommendation:* Approve chargeback.
* **If the customer failed to show up without cancelling per the merchant’s policy terms:**
    * *Recommendation:* Reject dispute. Merchant is entitled to the funds under "No-Show" terms.

## Risk Factors
* Travel agencies or airlines going through bankruptcy or mass cancellations.
* Subscription services making cancellation deliberately complex.

## Analyst Notes
Differentiate strictly between "Service Not Provided" and "Service Not As Described". If the service *was* provided but the customer is unhappy with the quality, this policy does not apply.
