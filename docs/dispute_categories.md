# Dispute Categories

## 1. Unauthorized Transaction

Definition:
A transaction performed without the cardholder's permission or knowledge.

Examples:

* Stolen card usage
* Card details compromised online
* Unauthorized international transactions

Keywords:
unauthorized, fraud, not me, stolen card, unknown transaction

---

## 2. Duplicate Charge

Definition:
The customer was charged multiple times for the same purchase.

Examples:

* Same transaction appears twice
* Merchant accidentally processed payment multiple times

Keywords:
charged twice, duplicate, double charge, multiple charges

---

## 3. Goods Not Received

Definition:
Customer paid for a product but never received it.

Examples:

* Package never delivered
* Delivery marked completed but item missing

Keywords:
not delivered, package missing, item not received

---

## 4. Service Not Provided

Definition:
Customer paid for a service that was never provided.

Examples:

* Flight cancelled without refund
* Hotel booking unavailable

Keywords:
service not provided, cancelled, refund requested

---

## 5. Billing Error

Definition:
Customer was charged an incorrect amount.

Examples:

* Wrong amount charged
* Unexpected fees

Keywords:
incorrect charge, billing error, overcharged

---

## 6. Merchant Fraud

Definition:
Merchant intentionally deceived the customer.

Examples:

* Fake online store
* Seller disappeared after payment

Keywords:
scam, fake seller, merchant fraud, deceptive business
